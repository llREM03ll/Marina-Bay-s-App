How it works

Every change is saved on the phone first, so the apps work without internet. When there is a connection, changes are copied to the cloud and the other phones get them.

Menu, inventory, recipes, counted cash, and expenses are synced. Printer settings, the PIN, and the open order stay on each phone.

If two phones change the same item, the one saved last wins. When another phone changes something you are looking at, a bar at the top asks you to refresh.
